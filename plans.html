Frequently Asked Subscription Questions

Can I switch between plans at any time?
Yes! You can upgrade or downgrade your plan directly in your Account Settings page. Upgrades take effect immediately, while downgrades reflect at the start of your next billing cycle.
Is there any long term contract or cancellation fee?
None whatsoever. StreamFlix operates purely on a month-to-month basis. You can cancel your subscription anytime with zero penalty or hidden cancellation fee.
What payment methods do you accept?
We accept all major credit and debit cards (Visa, MasterCard, American Express), PayPal, Apple Pay, Google Pay, and prepaid StreamFlix gift cards.
How does the 30-day trial work?
When you register for any plan, you receive 30 days of complimentary access. If you decide StreamFlix isn't right for you, cancel before day 30 and you won't be charged a single cent.
