Frequently Answered Questions

Click any topic to expand comprehensive step-by-step guidance.

1. How do I create an account and start my trial?
Visit our Sign Up page. Fill out your name, email, preferred password, date of birth, and country. Then choose your preferred plan on the Plans page. You'll receive instant access with your first 30 days on us.
2. What membership plans does StreamFlix provide?
We offer three distinct plans: Basic ($8.99/mo) with 720p HD and 1 screen; Standard ($13.99/mo) with 1080p Full HD and 2 screens; and Premium ($17.99/mo) with 4K Ultra HD, Dolby Atmos, and 4 concurrent screens.
3. How do I cancel my subscription?
You can cancel with zero hassle at any time. Go to Account Settings, locate the "Active Subscription" card, and click "Cancel Plan". Your access remains active until the end of your prepaid billing period.
4. Can I download movies and shows to watch offline?
Yes! On our Standard and Premium tiers, you can tap the download button on any title in the Details page or My List. Downloads remain playable offline for up to 30 days without an active internet connection.
5. How many screens can stream concurrently on one account?
Basic supports 1 screen, Standard supports 2 simultaneous screens, and Premium supports up to 4 simultaneous streams across smart TVs, laptops, consoles, or phones.
6. What devices and platforms are compatible?
StreamFlix is fully compatible with modern web browsers (Chrome, Edge, Firefox, Safari), Smart TVs (Samsung, LG, Sony, Android TV), Apple TV, Roku, Fire TV Stick, PlayStation 5, Xbox Series X, and iOS/Android mobile apps.
7. How do I reset or change my password?
If you are signed in, navigate to Account Security to input your current password and establish a new one. If you have forgotten your password, use the "Forgot Password" link on our Sign In page to receive a secure recovery email.
8. Why is video buffering or playing in low resolution?
StreamFlix uses adaptive streaming. Check that your connection is at least 5 Mbps for HD or 25 Mbps for 4K Ultra HD. You can also manually adjust your preferred streaming bitrate in Account Preferences.
9. How do I manage or add user profiles for family?
Visit the Profiles Screen. Each account supports up to 5 profiles, including dedicated Kids profiles with restricted age ratings and customizable parental PIN controls.
10. What is StreamFlix's refund policy?
If you experience technical issues or are dissatisfied within the first 14 days of an automatic billing cycle, contact our 24/7 billing support team through the contact options below to request a full refund.
11. How do I update my payment credit card or PayPal?
Visit Account Settings, click the "Subscription" card, and select "Update Payment Method". All transactions are protected by 256-bit SSL encryption.
12. Is there a free trial period?
Yes! Every new account registration begins with a 30-day complimentary trial with all premium features unlocked. You can cancel at any moment during the trial without paying anything.
